🎯 How to Play

Follow these simple steps to become a Snake master!

1

Press Start Game to begin playing.

2

Use Arrow Keys or WASD on desktop.

3

On mobile, swipe in any direction on the game board or use direction buttons.

4

Eat the glowing food to grow longer and increase your score.

5

Avoid hitting the walls or your own body.

6

Try to beat your highest score!
