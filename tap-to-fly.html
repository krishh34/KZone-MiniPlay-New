🎯 How to Play

Control your bird, avoid the pipes, and score as high as possible!

1

Click the Start Game button to begin playing.

2

Tap the screen, click your mouse, or press SPACE to make the bird fly upward.

3

Release between taps to let the bird move downward.

4

Carefully fly through the gaps between the pipes.

5

Avoid hitting the pipes or the boundaries of the game area.

6

Pass through pipes to increase your score and beat your best score!
