Combine numbers and try to create the 2048 tile!

1

Use the Arrow Keys on your keyboard to move the tiles.

2

Tiles with the same number combine when they touch.

3

Each successful move adds a new tile to the board.

4

Keep combining matching numbers to make bigger tiles.

5

Reach the 2048 tile to achieve the goal!

6

The game ends when no more moves are possible.
